Why does it still sound a bit robotic?
Safari on iPad only hands websites Apple's standard built-in voices. The higher-quality "Enhanced" and "Premium" voices you can download under Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content are used by VoiceOver and Speak Screen, but on iPadOS 18 they are generally not offered to websites, so downloading one usually will not change what you hear here. Three things genuinely do help, and all are free:

• Try every voice in the list. The gap between the best and worst is much larger than any slider.
• Leave pitch at 1.00. Pushing pitch up is what adds the metallic edge. It is only here in case a particular voice needs a nudge.
• Keep speed near 0.95. Slowing these voices down stretches them and makes them sound worse, not clearer.

If none of them are good enough, switch the voice off in Settings below and read the words to him yourself — the game stays fully playable, and it shows every prompt on screen instead.
